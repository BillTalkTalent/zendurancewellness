About Meghan

I've been on the calls. I know what you carry home.

I'm a licensed mental health counselor and licensed paramedic. My path to therapy ran through emergency medicine, public health, healthcare and crisis response — and I've seen firsthand how what we carry shows up in our bodies, relationships, work and identity.

Therapy with me is collaborative, practical and built around you. There's no one right way to heal, and I'm not interested in putting anyone in a box. Together we look at the whole person — mind and body.
