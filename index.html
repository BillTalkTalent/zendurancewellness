About Meghan

I understand burnout and overwhelm. You don't have to navigate them alone.

I'm a licensed mental health clinician and licensed paramedic. My path to therapy includes emergency medicine, public health, healthcare and crisis response — and I've seen firsthand how what we carry shows up in our bodies, relationships, work and identity.

Therapy, integration and coaching with me are collaborative, practical and built around you. There's no one right way to heal, and I'm not interested in putting anyone in a box. Together we look at the whole person — mind and body.

FAQ

Questions people ask before starting

What services does Zendurance Wellness offer?

Individual therapy for adults ages 18+, couples therapy for all relationships (including LGBTQIA+ affirming care), ketamine-assisted therapy, and non-clinical performance coaching.

Who is Meghan Fanning?

Meghan Fanning MA, NRP, LMHC/LCMHC is a licensed mental health clinician in New Hampshire and Massachusetts and a Nationally Registered Paramedic, with more than 20 years in emergency medicine, crisis response and mental health.

Who does Meghan work with?

Leaders and high achievers, veterans and first responders, healthcare professionals, and people facing death, dying and grief. Her clients are often the people others rely on.

Are sessions in person or online?

Services are offered via secure telehealth.

Is psychedelic-assisted therapy legal?

The legality of psychedelic-assisted therapy depends on the substance used and the laws in your state. Some treatments are legally available under specific medical guidelines, while others remain restricted or are only permitted in approved research settings.

I offer ketamine-assisted therapy in collaboration with a qualified provider. If you’d like to learn more about psychedelic-assisted therapy and whether it may be right for you, please reach out to me.

Can I work with Meghan if I live outside Massachusetts or New Hampshire?

Coaching and services are available anywhere in the United States and internationally via secure telehealth. Unlike therapy, coaching is not limited to specific states.

Do you take insurance?

Zendurance Wellness is a private-pay practice. HSA and FSA payments are accepted, and superbills are provided for out-of-network reimbursement.

How do I get started?

Use the Book Now button to register through the client portal, or use a Learn more button to email Meghan with questions about a service.

What should I do in a crisis?

If you are in immediate danger, call 911. For mental health crisis support, call or text 988.
